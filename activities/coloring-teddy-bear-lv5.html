About this printable

Hard antistress black and white coloring page with a teddy bear for experts and adults free to print

This printable is designed for easy use at home, in the classroom or while travelling. Download the full-size A4 worksheet or print it directly from this page.

What children can practice

Coloring gives children a relaxed way to practise hand control, careful movement inside outlines, observation of shapes and creative choices with colours.

Why this level?

Level 5 is the most detailed and demanding level, created for older children, teenagers and adults who enjoy longer creative challenges.

How to use this worksheet

  1. Print the black-and-white worksheet on A4 paper.
  2. Choose crayons, pencils or markers that suit the paper.
  3. Let the child colour freely; there is no required colour scheme.

Print & download

The worksheet is provided free for personal and educational use. For the clearest result, print at 100% scale or choose “fit to page” if your printer requires it.
