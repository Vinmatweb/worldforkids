Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

VinMat's World for Kids is an independent project offering printable activities for children. The public website does not require registration or user accounts, and visitors do not need to provide personal information to browse, print or download worksheets.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use that information only for communication connected with your request.

2. Analytics

The website may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, such as which pages are visited and how the site performs. Depending on your location, browser settings and consent choices, Google may process technical information such as device and browser information, approximate location, IP-derived information and interactions with pages.

3. Advertising, cookies and similar technologies

The website may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, web beacons or similar technologies for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes. Where required, a consent message may be shown before optional advertising or analytics technologies are used.

You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads settings and learn how Google uses data from partner sites in Google's privacy documentation.

4. Functional browser storage

Some features may use browser local storage for functional preferences such as language or interface settings. These values remain in your browser until you clear them.

5. Children's privacy

The worksheets are made for children, but the website is intended to be selected, downloaded and managed by parents, guardians, teachers and other adults. We do not knowingly ask children to create accounts, submit profiles or provide personal information.

6. Third-party links and services

The website may link to services such as Google, YouTube, Instagram or other external websites. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

7. Data retention and security

VinMat does not maintain a visitor account database. Email correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to messages and maintain normal project communication. Third-party analytics and advertising providers retain data according to their own policies and settings.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to vinmatforkids@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, legal requirements or third-party services change. The current revision date is shown at the top of this page.

Important: This Privacy Policy page itself intentionally contains no Google AdSense or Google Analytics tag.
